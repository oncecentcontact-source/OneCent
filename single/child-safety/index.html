Child Safety Standards

Last updated: April 4, 2026  |  Applies to: Ours Days (com.ours.calender.app)

Our Commitment

🛡️ Zero Tolerance Policy

Ours Days has a strict zero-tolerance policy against Child Sexual Abuse and Exploitation (CSAE). We are fully committed to protecting minors and ensuring our platform is never used to exploit, harm, or endanger children in any way. Any content, behavior, or activity that exploits or endangers minors is strictly prohibited and will result in immediate termination of access and reporting to the appropriate authorities.

👤 App Audience & Eligibility

Ours Days is designed exclusively for adults aged 18 and above. The app is a private, offline couples journal for tracking relationship milestones, saving memories, and daily mood check-ins.

  • Users must be 18 years of age or older to use this app.
  • The app is not directed at, marketed to, or intended for use by minors.
  • No features are designed for or accessible by children.

🚫 Prohibited Content & Behavior

The following content and behaviors are strictly prohibited on Ours Days:

  • Any content that sexually exploits or endangers minors (CSAM).
  • Grooming, solicitation, or any predatory behavior targeting minors.
  • Any attempt to use the app to harm, exploit, or endanger a child.
  • Sharing, uploading, or distributing illegal content involving minors.

🔒 Data & Privacy Protection

  • Ours Days operates 100% offline — no data is transmitted to external servers.
  • All data (photos, notes, memories) is stored locally on the user's device only.
  • No user-generated content is shared between users or uploaded to any cloud service.
  • No personal data of any kind is collected from users.

📢 Reporting Concerns

If you become aware of any child safety concern related to our app, we urge you to report it immediately. We take all reports seriously and will cooperate fully with law enforcement and relevant authorities.

  • Reports are reviewed promptly.
  • We cooperate with regional and national authorities on all child safety matters.
  • Violations are reported to the National Center for Missing & Exploited Children (NCMEC) and other relevant bodies as required by law.

⚖️ Legal Compliance

Ours Days complies with all applicable child safety laws and regulations, including but not limited to:

  • The Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA)
  • The PROTECT Our Children Act
  • Google Play's Child Safety Policy
  • All regional and national laws regarding child protection and CSAE prevention
